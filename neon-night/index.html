Ramen after the last train

A 14-stool ramen bar under Canal Street. Buy a ticket at the machine, sit down, eat. Open until 4am on Friday and Saturday.

Twelve buttons, one machine

The machine is by the door. Press a button, pay, and hand your ticket over the counter. Bowls arrive in about six minutes.

Open when the trains stop

The kitchen takes its last ticket 30 minutes before closing. On Monday we close to cook the broth for the week.

Fourteen stools, one long counter

No tables and no bookings. The pork broth simmers for 18 hours and the noodles are made each afternoon. When a stool opens, it is yours.
