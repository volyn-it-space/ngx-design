The private floor

A restaurant and three private rooms on the eleventh floor of 11 Meridian Row. Open five evenings a week, by reservation.

Reserve a table See the private rooms

Three rooms with a door that closes

Each room is given to one party for the whole evening.

  • The Meridian Room

    12 seats at one oval table

    For board dinners and family occasions. The room has its own door from the lift lobby and one captain for the whole evening.

    Minimum spend $2,400

  • The Card Room

    6 seats, low chairs

    For the hour after dinner. Leather chairs, a glass table, digestifs from the trolley and a door that closes.

    Minimum spend $600

  • The Cellar Table

    8 seats among 1,400 bottles

    For tasting dinners. The head sommelier opens each bottle at the table and pours it against the course it was chosen for.

    From $290 per guest

What the concierge arranges

One person looks after your evening, from the first call to the car home.

  • A car, both ways

    A driver collects your party and waits. Ask when you reserve, or by 3pm on the day.

  • Flowers for the table

    Arranged by our florist to your brief and in the room an hour before you arrive.

  • Cellar selections

    The sommelier sends a short list ahead. Chosen bottles are decanted before you sit down.

  • A menu for the evening

    The kitchen writes a menu around your guests, with allergies and preferences noted in advance.

  • A private arrival

    The side entrance on Meridian Row and a separate lift, for guests who prefer not to be seen.

  • One account

    A single invoice after the evening. No bill is brought to the table.

Reservations

Call the floor between noon and 10pm. For a private room, write to us with the date and the number of guests. We reply the same day.
