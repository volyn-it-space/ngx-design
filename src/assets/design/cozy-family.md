# Cozy Family Design Brief


## Visual Direction

Comfortable, warm, trustworthy, and easy to scan. The page should make families feel that the place is relaxed, practical, and welcoming - bright cream walls, golden yellow accents, coral-red highlights, chocolate-brown text.

## Theme Mode

- **Theme mode:** light
- **Why:** the warm cream/coral/yellow palette communicates daytime, family-friendly comfort. On dark it inverts to a moodier dinner setting that contradicts the brand promise.

## Theme Colors

Map these colors onto the target project's existing theme tokens, CSS variables, design tokens, global styles, stylesheet variables, or component styles. If the project has no theme system, introduce the smallest local color structure needed for this design.

- background primary: `#FFF6E8` (warm cream)
- background secondary: `#FFFBF2`
- background tertiary: `#FBEFD8`
- text strong: `#6E4325` (chocolate brown, 6.7:1 on cream)
- text: `#5A4030`
- text muted: `#7D6048` (5.6:1 on cream)
- primary accent: `#B5472A` (deep coral, for text, links and buttons; `#D86D4D` for borders only)
- secondary accent: `#F7C968` (golden yellow - secondary accent)
- border: `#EAD8B8`
- medium shadow: `0 8px 22px rgba(138, 90, 58, 0.12)`
- focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(216, 109, 77, 0.40)`


## Typography

- **Display:** `DM Serif Display` (Google Fonts, weight 400), fallback `Georgia, serif`.
- **Body:** `Inter` (Google Fonts, weights 400, 500, 600, 700), fallback `system-ui, sans-serif`. 16px minimum, line-height 1.65, nothing under 13px.
- **Loading:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Serif+Display&family=Inter:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap`
- **Voice:** warm and practical. Prices, high chairs, kids deals. Short sentences.

## Section Anatomy

- **Header:** coral wordmark and four text links.
- **Hero:** left, headline, lede and two actions; right, three white-bordered snapshots overlapping and slightly rotated (landscape, portrait, small landscape). The stack is square and capped at 30rem on a phone.
- **This week:** tan rounded panel with seven day cards (day, special, price) with a yellow top border; two columns on a phone with Sunday spanning both.
- **For families:** tall rounded photo (4:5) beside three fact blocks (kids price, high chairs, little plates first) with coral left borders.
- **Our story:** two short paragraphs beside an arched tall photo (4:5).
- **Visit:** yellow rounded panel: heading, phone button and an opening-hours table in dark brown (above 7:1 on yellow).
- **Footer:** wordmark, fictional venue line, link back to the catalog.

## Photography

- Daylight and warm light, shared plates, hands, checkered cloths. No filter.
- The demo hotlinks Pexels photos. Replace them with the venue own and keep the crops (10:7, 4:5 and 4:3 snapshots, 4:5 fact and story).

## Avoid

- Premium fine-dining gloss, heavy effects, or dense copy.
- Hiding hours, phone, or address behind expand/click.
- Mood photography or dark interior shots.
- "Luxurious", "exclusive", "curated" - wrong register for this brand.
