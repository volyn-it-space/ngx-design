# Event Banquet Design Brief


## Visual Direction

Polished, celebratory, organized, and inquiry-led. The page should sell event formats, capacity, packages, and the inquiry flow - ivory backgrounds, deep navy structure, champagne gold detail, cream linen surfaces.

## Theme Mode

- **Theme mode:** light
- **Why:** ivory + navy + champagne gold is built for daylit weddings and gala brochures. The dark variant works for evening galas but a sales-focused inquiry page is more effective on a bright ground.

## Theme Colors

Map these colors onto the target project's existing theme tokens, CSS variables, design tokens, global styles, stylesheet variables, or component styles. If the project has no theme system, introduce the smallest local color structure needed for this design.

- background primary: `#FEFDFB` (ivory)
- background secondary: `#FAF5EB` (cream)
- background tertiary: `#F2E8D0`
- text strong: `#1A2847` (deep navy)
- text: `#2A3552`
- text muted: `#596079` (6.4:1 on ivory)
- primary accent: `#1A2847` (deep navy - primary accent)
- secondary accent: `#D4AF37` (champagne gold, for rules and on navy only, never as text on light)
- border: `#E6D9BD`
- medium shadow: `0 12px 28px rgba(26, 40, 71, 0.10)`
- focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)`


## Typography

- **Display:** `Playfair Display` (Google Fonts, weights 600, 700), fallback `Georgia, serif`.
- **Body:** `Lora` (Google Fonts, weights 400, 500), fallback `Georgia, serif`. 16px minimum, line-height 1.7, nothing under 13px.
- **Loading:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Playfair+Display:wght@600;700&family=Lora:wght@400;500&display=swap`
- **Voice:** a planner: capacities, hire prices, per-guest prices, next steps.

## Section Anatomy

- **Header:** navy serif wordmark and four text links.
- **Hero:** a wide hall photo (15:7), then headline left and lede with two actions right, then a navy capacity bar with three gold numerals (320 seated, 3 halls, 120 cars). Stacks on a phone.
- **Halls:** a real table (hall with 4:3 photo, seated, standing, size, hire from) on desktop. Under 40rem each row becomes a stacked card with a full-width photo and labelled values.
- **Packages:** three tiers (Gathering, Celebration, Gala) with a gold top border, a large from-price per guest and an included list; two 7:4.8 table photos below.
- **How it works:** four numbered steps (enquire, visit, plan, celebrate) under navy rules.
- **Enquire:** navy panel with the call to action: phone button in gold, email link and address.
- **Footer:** wordmark, fictional venue line, link back to the catalog.

## Photography

- Daylight and soft light, round tables, linen, flowers. No filter.
- The demo hotlinks Pexels photos. Replace them with the venue own and keep the crops (15:7 hero, 4:3 halls, 7:4.8 tables).

## Avoid

- Generic stock wedding photography without venue context.
- Hiding pricing entirely - at least give bands or starting points.
- Hard-sell language; clients are already shopping when they land.
- Mixed-purpose CTAs (book a table + book a wedding) on the same surface.
