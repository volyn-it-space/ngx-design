# Classic Elegant Design Brief


## Visual Direction

Timeless, polished, formal, and reliable. The page should feel like a long-standing hotel dining room - ivory walls, warm-brown wood, antique-gold accents, muted-taupe shadow. Nothing modern shouts here.

## Theme Mode

- **Theme mode:** light
- **Why:** ivory + warm brown + antique gold is a daylit interior palette. The "timeless premium" register reads on light; on dark it tips into nightclub luxury.

## Typography

- **Display:** `Cormorant Garamond` (Google Fonts, weights 500, 600), fallback `Georgia, serif`. Uppercase wordmark with tracking, italic section titles on the carte.
- **Body:** `Lora` (Google Fonts, weights 400, 500), fallback `Georgia, serif`. 16px minimum, line-height 1.7, nothing under 13px.
- **Loading:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Cormorant+Garamond:wght@500;600&family=Lora:wght@400;500&display=swap`
- **Voice:** formal and calm. French menu names with an English note.

## Section Anatomy

- **Header:** centered uppercase wordmark with a centered nav row on a hairline.
- **Hero:** everything centered: headline, a short double rule with a gold lozenge, lede, two actions, then a landscape photo (1100:620) in a thin gold frame.
- **A la carte:** centered heading and rule; two columns (to begin, to follow) of four dishes each, name and italic note on the left, price on the right, on hairlines; one lunch-menu line below.
- **Private room:** tan band: text and three numerals (seated, reception, maitre d) beside two photos (3:2 and 3:4).
- **Our house:** gold-framed tall photo (3:4) beside two paragraphs of history.
- **Reservations:** centered heading, rule, phone button and a details list (hours, address, dress, wine).
- **Footer:** centered wordmark, fictional venue line, link back to the catalog.

## Photography

- Daylight on linen and chandeliers; formal, symmetrical. No filter.
- The demo hotlinks Pexels photos. Replace them with the restaurant own and keep the crops (1100:620 hero, 3:2 and 3:4 private room, 3:4 history).

## Avoid

- Modern minimalist tropes (extreme whitespace, micro-typography).
- Casual or playful illustrations.
- Marketing claims about "trends" - this design is anti-trend.
- Cluttered card grids; favor classical column rhythm.
