# Boutique Hotel Design Brief


## Visual Direction

Atmospheric, curated, and personal. The page should sell rooms, atmosphere, location, amenities, and direct booking - ivory and warm-charcoal interior palette, muted-rose accent, soft warm-gray detail. Quiet design, not corporate hotel chain.

## Theme Mode

- **Theme mode:** light
- **Why:** ivory and warm charcoal is a daylit guest-experience palette. The brand promise is "personalized stays" which reads warmer on light surfaces.

## Theme Colors

Map these colors onto the target project's existing theme tokens, CSS variables, design tokens, global styles, stylesheet variables, or component styles. If the project has no theme system, introduce the smallest local color structure needed for this design.

- background primary: `#FAF9F7` (ivory)
- background secondary: `#FFFFFF`
- background tertiary: `#F2EFEA`
- text strong: `#2C2C2C`
- text: `#444444`
- text muted: `#6B6B66` (5.2:1 on ivory)
- primary accent: `#3D3D3D` (warm charcoal - primary accent)
- secondary accent: `#C4B5AC` (muted rose, for rules, underlines and on charcoal only)
- border: `#E4DED5`
- medium shadow: `0 12px 28px rgba(61, 61, 61, 0.10)`
- focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(196, 181, 172, 0.45)`


## Typography

- **Display and body:** `Inter` (Google Fonts, weights 400, 500, 600), fallback `system-ui, sans-serif`. Headings weight 500 with -0.02em tracking. 16px minimum, line-height 1.65, nothing under 13px.
- **Loading:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@400;500;600&display=swap`
- **Voice:** quiet and practical. Sizes, rates, walking minutes.

## Section Anatomy

- **Header:** uppercase wordmark and four text links.
- **Hero:** left, headline, lede, two actions and a plain-text facts strip (check-in, check-out, rooms, neighbourhood); right, a tall room photo (18:25). On a phone the photo moves first at 4:3.
- **Rooms:** three alternating rows (photo 3:2 and text, mirrored on every second row) with name, size, a line and a from-price per night.
- **The area:** tan panel with five numbered places: name, note and walking minutes on rose hairlines.
- **Amenities:** tall photo (4:5) beside a two-column list of eight inclusions.
- **Book:** charcoal panel with phone button (rose), email link and address.
- **Footer:** wordmark, fictional venue line, link back to the catalog.

## Photography

- Bright daylight rooms in neutral tones. No filter.
- The demo hotlinks Pexels photos. Replace them with the hotel own and keep the crops (18:25 hero, 3:2 rooms, 4:5 amenities).

## Avoid

- Big-chain stock photography of generic rooms.
- Hidden cancellation terms - surface the policy in the booking section.
- Loyalty-program prompts before the guest even sees rooms.
- "Luxury" framing if the venue is actually boutique/personal - different register.
