It started at one kitchen table

Rosa cooked Sunday lunch for twelve cousins, and the neighbours kept turning up. In 2009 her son opened the doors on Orchard Street so everyone could have a chair.

The recipes are still hers. The sauce takes four hours, and the kids menu is made from the same pots as everyone else.

Come as you are, bring everyone
