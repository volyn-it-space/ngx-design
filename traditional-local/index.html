Recipes older than the oven they are baked in.

Village cooking from three generations of one family. Soups, roasts and bread, all made on a wood fire.

Four dishes we have always cooked

The same recipes since 1952, made with the same patience.

  • Harvest soup in a bread bowl

    $13

    Root vegetables and smoked bacon, served in a loaf baked that morning.

  • Pot-roast of the house

    $24

    Beef shoulder braised six hours with onions, paprika and a little red wine.

  • Hearth loaf with butter

    $7

    Rye and wheat sourdough baked in the stone oven, with salted butter.

  • Village flatbread

Seventy years, four dates

  1. 1952

    Marta opens a bakery in the front room of the family house.

  2. 1971

    The stone oven is built, and the first dinners are served at two tables.

  3. 1998

    The second generation moves the kitchen into the old hay barn.

  4. 2019

    The third generation restores the hearth and writes the recipes down.
