Out of the oven today

Four bakes a day. Come at the time that suits the thing you want.

  1. 7:00

    Butter croissants and pain au chocolat

    Shaped the evening before, baked as the door opens.

  2. 9:00

    Country sourdough and seeded rye

    A two-day dough. Ask and we will slice it for you.

  3. 11:30

    Cinnamon buns and cardamom knots

    Still warm at lunch. Usually gone by two.

  4. 14:00

    Rosemary focaccia and oat cookies

    The last bake of the day, in time for the school run.
