A day at Still Water

There is no schedule. This is only what a good day can look like.

  1. 7:00

    Sunrise stretch

    A slow group class on the terrace, with tea afterwards.

  2. 9:30

    Breakfast by the water

    Fruit, eggs and warm bread, eaten without hurry.

  3. 12:00

    Your treatment

    Book any time between noon and five. Robes and slippers are in your room.

  4. 16:00

    The quiet pool

    Adults only after four. No phones, and no music.

  5. 19:30

    Supper and sleep

    A light menu in the garden room, then turn-down at nine.
