# Resort Spa Design Brief


## Visual Direction

Calm, breathable, natural, and restorative. The page should reduce friction and communicate quiet, care, and escape - sage and teal greens, sky-blue softness, sand neutrals, natural light through every surface.

## Theme Mode

- **Theme mode:** light
- **Why:** the sage/teal/sand palette is built around daylight and natural materials. Dark mode breaks the "calm and breathable" promise that the brand is selling.

## Theme Colors

Map these colors onto the target project's existing theme tokens, CSS variables, design tokens, global styles, stylesheet variables, or component styles. If the project has no theme system, introduce the smallest local color structure needed for this design.

- background primary: `#F4EFE3` (sand cream)
- background secondary: `#FBF7EE`
- background tertiary: `#E8DCC8` (sand)
- text strong: `#223B2D`
- text: `#364C3F`
- text muted: `#566A5B` (5.6:1 on sand cream)
- primary accent: `#2D4A3A` (deep green, for buttons and text; sage `#7FA88D` is for rules and decoration only)
- secondary accent: `#3F7D6D` (teal, darkened for text)
- border: `#D4C9B2`
- medium shadow: `0 12px 26px rgba(45, 74, 58, 0.10)`
- focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(127, 168, 141, 0.45)`


## Typography

- **Display:** `Fraunces` (Google Fonts, weight 500, italic 500), fallback `Georgia, serif`.
- **Body:** `Inter` (Google Fonts, weights 400, 500, 600), fallback `system-ui, sans-serif`. 16px minimum, line-height 1.7, nothing under 13px.
- **Loading:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces:ital,wght@0,500;1,500&family=Inter:wght@400;500;600&display=swap`
- **Voice:** unhurried. Minutes, prices and what is quiet. No claims about results.

## Section Anatomy

- **Header:** serif wordmark and four text links.
- **Hero:** a narrow text column (headline, lede, two actions) beside a very tall arched photo (18:25) of the pool by the sea. On a phone the photo comes first, capped at 22rem.
- **Treatments:** two lists (massage and body, face and rest) of four treatments each: name, minutes and price on hairlines under a sage rule.
- **A day here:** a vertical timeline with a sage line and round markers: five moments from 7:00 to 19:30; two moments carry a 4:3 photo.
- **Stays:** three cards (2, 3 and 5 nights) with a from-price per person, then two 4:3 pool photos.
- **Book:** a full-width closing photo band (1400:620; 4:3 on a phone), then heading, text, a pill phone button and the address.
- **Footer:** wordmark, fictional venue line, link back to the catalog.

## Photography

- Soft daylight, water, stone, towels and flowers. No people as the subject, and no treatment-table photos. No filter.
- The demo hotlinks Pexels photos. Replace them with the resort own and keep the crops (18:25 hero, 4:3 moments and pool, 1400:620 closing band).

## Avoid

- Vague wellness language ("rejuvenate", "transformative experience") without concrete details.
- High-saturation tropical imagery (this is sage/teal, not beach club).
- Stock photography of generic massage tables.
- Heavy chrome or shiny luxury detail.
