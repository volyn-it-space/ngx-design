# Rustic Farmhouse Design Brief


## Visual Direction

Honest, grounded, seasonal, and rural. The page should feel like a converted barn at noon - cream walls, barn-brown beams, terracotta accents, sage-olive trim. Local produce, weather, and season are part of the brand.

## Theme Mode

- **Theme mode:** light
- **Why:** the cream/terracotta/olive palette is daylit by design. The brand is "countryside hospitality", which reads warmer and more grounded on a light ground.

## Theme Colors

Map these colors onto the target project's existing theme tokens, CSS variables, design tokens, global styles, stylesheet variables, or component styles. If the project has no theme system, introduce the smallest local color structure needed for this design.

- background primary: `#F5EDE3` (cream)
- background secondary: `#FBF6EA`
- background tertiary: `#EAD8C0`
- text strong: `#5E3A1C`
- text: `#46301C`
- text muted: `#75583F` (5.6:1 on cream)
- primary accent: `#9A5A2C` (terracotta, darkened for text and buttons; `#B8733C` for decoration only)
- secondary accent: `#5D6C3F` (olive, darkened for text)
- border: `#D9C5A8`
- medium shadow: `0 8px 20px rgba(107, 68, 35, 0.12)`
- focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(184, 115, 60, 0.45)`


## Typography

- **Display:** `Fraunces` (Google Fonts, weights 600, 700), fallback `Georgia, serif`.
- **Body:** `Lora` (Google Fonts, weights 400, 500, 600), fallback `Georgia, serif`. 16px minimum, line-height 1.7, nothing under 13px.
- **Loading:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces:wght@600;700&family=Lora:wght@400;500;600&display=swap`
- **Voice:** a farmer talking. Seasons, distances in km, what came in this morning.

## Section Anatomy

- **Header:** terracotta wordmark and four text links.
- **Hero:** copy on the left; right, a collage of three square photos with thick paper-like borders, overlapping.
- **What is growing:** a four-column seasons calendar (spring, summer, autumn, winter) with months, what grows and a dish; autumn has a terracotta border. Two columns, then one.
- **Our neighbours:** four suppliers with what they give and distance in km, on hairlines.
- **The table:** three blocks (long table, pantry shelf, market box), each a square paper-bordered photo with a title and line.
- **Visit:** olive-bordered panel: text and phone button, address, directions, hours and parking.
- **Footer:** wordmark, fictional venue line, link back to the catalog.

## Photography

- Daylight, vegetables, jars and wood. Warm and a little earthy. No filter; prints have a thick cream border and a soft shadow.
- The demo hotlinks Pexels photos. Replace them with the farm own and keep the square (1:1) crops.

## Avoid

- Generic "farmhouse" branding without specific producers or seasonal items.
- High-gloss food styling - this brand is honest, slightly imperfect, real.
- Decorative chalkboard-script fonts or barnyard clip art.
- Hiding the rural location - surface directions and driving guidance.
