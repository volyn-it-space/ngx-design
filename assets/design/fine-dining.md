# Fine Dining Design Brief


## Visual Direction

Refined, editorial, precise, quiet, and premium. The page should create confidence through restraint, strong hierarchy, and carefully selected imagery - champagne ivory walls, deep navy accents, burnished gold detail.

## Theme Mode

- **Theme mode:** light
- **Why:** the palette is ivory-dominant. Navy and gold work as editorial accents on a champagne-ivory ground; light is the brand's resting state. Dark mode is a viable evening variant but should not be the default.

## Theme Colors

Map these colors onto the target project's existing theme tokens, CSS variables, design tokens, global styles, stylesheet variables, or component styles. If the project has no theme system, introduce the smallest local color structure needed for this design.

- background primary: `#FAF8F3` (champagne ivory)
- background secondary: `#FFFFFF`
- background tertiary: `#F2EEE3`
- text strong: `#1A1A2E` (deep navy)
- text: `#2E2E38`
- text muted: `#5C5C66` (6.4:1 on ivory)
- primary accent: `#1A1A2E` (deep navy - primary accent)
- secondary accent: `#C9A961` (burnished gold, for rules and borders only, never text)
- border: `#E2DCCB`
- medium shadow: `0 10px 30px rgba(26, 26, 46, 0.10)`
- focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(201, 169, 97, 0.45)`


## Typography

- **Display:** `Playfair Display` (Google Fonts, weights 500, 600), fallback `Georgia, serif`.
- **Body:** `Lora` (Google Fonts, weights 400, 500), fallback `Georgia, serif`. 16px minimum, line-height 1.7, nothing under 13px.
- **Loading:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Playfair+Display:wght@500;600&family=Lora:wght@400;500&display=swap`
- **Voice:** editorial and restrained. Courses, producers, one price.

## Section Anatomy

- **Header:** navy serif wordmark and four text links on a hairline.
- **Hero:** a wide plated-dish photo (15:7; 4:3 on a phone) with the headline panel below it, overlapping the photo by 3.5rem: white panel, gold top rule, centered headline, lede and two actions.
- **Tasting menu:** one narrow centered column: seven courses with a small number, name and italic note on hairlines, then one price line with a gold double rule.
- **The kitchen:** tall photo (4:5) beside a note from the kitchen and two small 7:5 plate photos.
- **Wine:** a real pairing table (with, wine, region) with a gold header rule.
- **Reservations:** white panel with a gold top rule: text and phone button, and seatings, address, dress, allergies.
- **Footer:** wordmark, fictional venue line, link back to the catalog.

## Photography

- Daylight, white plates, quiet backgrounds. No filter.
- The demo hotlinks Pexels photos. Replace them with the restaurant own and keep the crops (15:7 hero, 4:5 kitchen, 7:5 plates).

## Avoid

- Playful decorative UI, overloaded cards, loud colors, or casual copy.
- Stock food photography or overly saturated dish shots.
- "Award-winning", "renowned", "best" - let the chef and menu speak.
- Family-friendly framing.
