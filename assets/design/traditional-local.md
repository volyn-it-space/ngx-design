# Traditional Local Design Brief


## Visual Direction

Heritage, regional, authentic, and grounded. The page should feel like a long-standing country tavern - wheat-cream walls, burgundy red highlights, forest-green tradition, antique gold trim. Pride of place is the design language.

## Theme Mode

- **Theme mode:** light
- **Why:** the wheat/burgundy/forest palette is built around a daytime tavern interior. On dark it tips into theatrical "heritage restaurant" branding that loses the lived-in feel.

## Theme Colors

Map these colors onto the target project's existing theme tokens, CSS variables, design tokens, global styles, stylesheet variables, or component styles. If the project has no theme system, introduce the smallest local color structure needed for this design.

- background primary: `#F4E8C8` (wheat cream)
- background secondary: `#FBF4DD`
- background tertiary: `#EAD3AA`
- text strong: `#3A2A1E`
- text: `#4D3828`
- text muted: `#6F5538` (5.6:1 on wheat)
- primary accent: `#8E281F` (burgundy red - primary accent)
- secondary accent: `#2F5B3D` (forest green - secondary accent)
- border: `#D3B88F`
- medium shadow: `0 8px 20px rgba(90, 64, 48, 0.12)`
- focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(178, 123, 44, 0.45)`


## Typography

- **Display:** `Playfair Display` (Google Fonts, weights 600, 700), fallback `Georgia, serif`.
- **Body:** `Lora` (Google Fonts, weights 400, 500, 600), fallback `Georgia, serif`. 16px minimum, line-height 1.7, nothing under 13px.
- **Loading:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Playfair+Display:wght@600;700&family=Lora:wght@400;500;600&display=swap`
- **Voice:** a family telling its own story. Dates, names, distances.

## Section Anatomy

- **Header:** burgundy wordmark, four text links, then a CSS-only folk pattern band (red and green diamonds on cream between two red rules).
- **Hero:** left, headline, lede and two actions; right, a tall hearth photo (4:5) in a double burgundy border. Stacks on a phone, photo capped at 24rem.
- **House recipes:** four dishes in two columns; each card has a photo (7:5) beside name, price and one line. Photo on top on a phone.
- **Four dates:** tan band with four milestones (1952, 1971, 1998, 2019), a red top rule and a large year each; two columns, then one.
- **From the region:** four suppliers with what they give and a distance in km, beside a tall photo (3:4) of the dining room.
- **Visit:** cream panel with a double green border: text, phone button and address, hours, tables and take-away.
- **Footer:** wordmark, fictional venue line, link back to the catalog.

## Photography

- Warm daylight and firelight: bread, pots, stone and wood. No filter.
- The demo hotlinks Pexels photos. Replace them with the venue own and keep the crops (4:5 hero, 7:5 dishes, 3:4 room).

## Avoid

- Pan-cultural "ethnic" decoration that flattens regional identity.
- Modernist tropes - this design is intentionally non-trendy.
- Stock photography of generic "old-world" interiors.
- Family-friendly framing if the venue is wine/spirit-led; calibrate to the real business.
