# Botanical Bar Design Brief


## Visual Direction

A greenhouse after dark. The page should feel like a glass conservatory at 10pm: deep forest walls, hanging foliage in silhouette, brass lamp glow, terracotta pots, cream linen napkins. Plants set the mood, cocktails are the medium.

## Theme Mode

- **Theme mode:** dark
- **Why:** the night-greenhouse metaphor is the whole point. Light mode collapses the after-dark conservatory feel into a generic plant-shop look. Forest green only registers as a room (not a brand color) on a dark surface.

## Theme Colors

Map these colors onto the target project's existing theme tokens, CSS variables, design tokens, global styles, stylesheet variables, or component styles. If the project has no theme system, introduce the smallest local color structure needed for this design.

- background primary: `#0F2A1E` (deep forest)
- background secondary: `#173A29`
- background tertiary: `#1F4533`
- text strong: `#F2EAD3` (linen cream)
- text: `#D2C7A6`
- text muted: `#A9B496` (6.4:1 on forest)
- primary accent: `#C9A574` (brass, lightened for contrast)
- secondary accent: `#E0895A` (terracotta, lightened for text on forest)
- border: `#1F4533`
- medium shadow: `0 10px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`
- focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(184, 153, 104, 0.40)`


## Typography

- **Display:** `Fraunces` (Google Fonts, weights 500, 600, italic 500), fallback `Georgia, serif`. Italic plant family names.
- **Body:** `Inter` (Google Fonts, weights 400, 500, 600), fallback `system-ui, sans-serif`. 16px minimum, line-height 1.65, nothing under 13px.
- **Loading:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces:ital,wght@0,500;0,600;1,500;1,600&family=Inter:wght@400;500;600&display=swap`
- **Voice:** a gardener who also makes drinks. Plant names, what is in season.

## Section Anatomy

- **Header:** italic brass wordmark and four text links.
- **Hero:** copy on the left; right, a photo in a soft organic blob (8:9) overlapped by a smaller round photo with a forest ring.
- **Drinks by plant family:** three columns (Herb, Flower, Root), each with an italic name, a terracotta note and three drinks with ingredients and price on hairlines.
- **Garden:** arched tall photo (4:5) beside heading, text and ten plant names as text chips.
- **Autumn:** rounded panel with the seasonal change on the left and two 4:5 photos on the right (second offset down).
- **Visit:** heading, text, phone button, and address, hours, garden and no-alcohol details.
- **Footer:** wordmark, fictional venue line, link back to the catalog.

## Photography

- Cocktails with fresh herbs, flowers and citrus. Dark-leaning, lightly muted (`saturate(0.9) brightness(0.92)`).
- The demo hotlinks Pexels photos. Replace them with the bar own and keep the crops (8:9 blob, 1:1 round, 4:5 arch, 4:5 season).

## Avoid

- Tropical / beach / summer-festival imagery.
- Bright daylight greenhouse photos - lean into night and lamp glow.
- Cliched leaf-pattern backgrounds. Use real plant photography or none.
- Wellness/yoga voice. This is a bar, not a wellness retreat.
